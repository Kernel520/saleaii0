5.0 — FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do we agree on a price? Is there a minimum?

The price is negotiable. Email your budget range and intended use. Reasonable offers will receive a considered reply, with priority given to serious offers.

What payment methods do you support?

Escrow.com is recommended, and major domain marketplaces are also supported. The buyer covers the escrow fee, subject to negotiation. Direct transfers without escrow are not accepted.

How long does the transfer take?

Once the escrow release conditions are met, a transfer between registrars usually takes 1–5 days. A push within the same registrar can be completed the same day. We’ll coordinate the DNS handoff with you in advance.

Can I lease the domain or pay in installments?

Long-term leases or installment plans may be possible depending on the proposal. Email to discuss the terms. Ownership is transferred after full payment.

Is there another way to get in touch?

Email is the main way to reach us: email@aii0.com. Include aii0.com in the subject line. We usually reply within 24 hours.
